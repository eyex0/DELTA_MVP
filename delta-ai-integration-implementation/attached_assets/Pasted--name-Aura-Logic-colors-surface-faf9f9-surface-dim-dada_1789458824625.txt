## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in **Extreme Minimalism** with a focus on high-end B2B precision. It balances the starkness of a monochrome utilitarian interface with the sophisticated warmth of subtle mesh gradients. The brand personality is professional, authoritative, and cutting-edge, catering to power users who value speed and clarity.

The visual narrative relies on "The Void"—generous negative space that directs focus toward core functional blocks. It blends **Corporate Modern** structure with **Vaporwave-inspired subtle accents**, using pure black and white as the primary canvas while reserving vibrant hues for highlights and status indicators.

## Colors

The palette is intentionally restricted to create a premium, high-contrast environment. 

- **Primary Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` is the base for all primary surfaces.
- **Action Surface:** Solid `#000000` is reserved for primary buttons, core branding, and high-emphasis text.
- **Secondary Surfaces:** `#F2F2F0` provides a soft distinction for sidebars, secondary backgrounds, and inactive states.
- **Accents:** Use mesh gradients (combining the named colors with low opacity) only for background blurs, empty states, or high-value feature highlights. These should never interfere with readability.

## Typography

This design system utilizes a trio of typefaces to establish a clear information hierarchy:
- **Geist** for headlines to provide a modern, technical, and sharp appearance.
- **Inter** for body copy to ensure maximum legibility and professional neutrality.
- **JetBrains Mono** for small labels, data points, and metadata to reinforce the sense of precision and "under-the-hood" power.

Keep line lengths for body text between 60-75 characters to maintain readability within the expansive white space.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for desktop to maintain a "contained" and high-end feel. 
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column grid with 64px outer margins and 24px gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with 40px margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid grid with 20px margins.

Spacing should be generous. Use a base 8px scale, favoring larger increments (48px, 64px, 80px) to separate functional blocks. Vertically, sections should breathe with gaps up to 120px to prevent visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids traditional drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** and **Subtle Outlines**.
- **Surface Elevation:** Use `#F2F2F0` to lift secondary containers above the `#FFFFFF` base.
- **Outlines:** Use 1px solid `#E5E5E2` for card borders and input fields.
- **Active State:** On hover, instead of a shadow, increase the border weight to 1.5px or shift the background color slightly.
- **Glassmorphism:** Reserved exclusively for navigation bars (top blur) or modal overlays using a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with a 70% white tint.

## Shapes

The design system employs a "Contained Curvature" strategy:
- **Outer Containers:** Large cards and main content areas use a radius of 24px to 32px.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons, tags, tabs, and toggle switches are **fully rounded (pill-shaped)**.
- **Consistency:** Never mix sharp corners with rounded corners. If an element is nested, its radius should be slightly smaller than the parent to maintain optical harmony.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are solid `#000000` with white text. Secondary buttons are outlined or light gray. All must be pill-shaped.
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist with a 1px bottom border or a subtle 1px gray frame. Use `label-mono` for field titles.
- **Cards:** White background with a 1px `#E5E5E2` border and 32px corner radius. No shadows.
- **Chips/Tabs:** Pill-shaped. Active tabs are solid black; inactive tabs are transparent with subtle text.
- **Mesh Accents:** Use as soft background blurs behind specific components (like a "Pro" feature card) to create a "halo" effect without breaking the minimalist grid.
- **Status Indicators:** Small 8px circles using the named accent colors (e.g., Pink for 'Live', Purple for 'Processing').
